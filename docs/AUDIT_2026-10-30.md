## What Phase 5 actually is
Not speculative decoding or early exit. It's **CUDA Graph reuse by KV-capacity bucket**:
`DecoderPool` (`research/phase5/adaptive_pool.py`) keys graphs by `(batch, capacity)`. `full` mode
uses one graph at `block_size=256`; `adaptive` picks the smallest bucket in (80,128,192,256)
that fits `prompt + max_new`. Decode step = Phase 4 `StaticKVDecoder.step` (masked fixed-size SDPA,
GPU-resident position) captured once per key; prefill is eager. Greedy only, no sampling, no EOS,
all rows in a batch share one prompt length. Intended contract: **exact** greedy-token equality with
`generate_reference` under the same precision policy.

**Noise floor:** at prompt=192 both modes use capacity 256, i.e. the same work, yet they differ by up to 8%.
Treat full-vs-adaptive differences below roughly 8% as unresolved. Gains of 18–47% at small
capacity (80/128) showed up in all three independent runs (old log, quick run, T3), but none has
a confidence interval. Performance classification: **INCONCLUSIVE** under the 5% + CI promotion rule. Likely real where ≥18%.
